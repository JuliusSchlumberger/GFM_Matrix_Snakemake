Sensitivity of the FES version rule to its two thresholds. The rule replaces FES2022 by FES2014 at a
point where MHWS_FES2022 > R x MHWS_FES2014 and MHWS_FES2022 - MHWS_FES2014 > D. Each cell is one
(R, D) pair: R on the vertical axis, D on the horizontal axis. The red frame marks the adopted pair,
R = 1.5 and D = 1.25 m.

(a) The rule applied at the GESLA tide gauges (3 firings at the adopted pair; 905 gauges with MHWS
from GESLA, FES2022 and FES2014). The number in a cell is how many gauges the rule fires at
("fires": FES2014 would be used there). Each firing is then scored against the gauge's own MHWS: it
is a fix if FES2014 is closer to the gauge than FES2022 by more than 5 cm, a harm if it is further
by more than 5 cm, and neutral otherwise. The cell colour is the number of harms (white = none). A
good pair fires only where FES2022 is badly wrong: few firings, all of them fixes, no harm. Fixes at
the adopted pair: 3; harms: 0.

(b) The rule applied at the 178 known artefacts: boundary nodes in the four basins of fig05 whose
FES2022 MHWS exceeds 1.25 x the largest MHWS observed in that basin (Minas 8.15 m, Strait of
Magellan 5.5 m, Colorado mouth 5.0 m, Lynn Canal 2.8 m; half the record or upper spring range, and
the Skagway gauge). The factor 1.25 is a margin on the observed value, not a threshold of the rule:
it defines a reference set of nodes that are wrong without looking at FES2014, so the test is not
circular. The number in a cell is how many of the 178 the rule replaces, the colour that share
(white 0%, dark blue 100%). A good pair replaces all of them.

Why D matters most: comparing the two atlases at the gauges, binned by how much FES2022 exceeds
FES2014 in MHWS. The gain of switching at a gauge is |FES2022 - GESLA| - |FES2014 - GESLA| in MHWS:
positive when FES2014 is the better value (-inf .. -0.25 m: n=29, FES2014 closer at 31%, median gain
-0.34 m; -0.25 .. -0.10 m: n=32, FES2014 closer at 44%, median gain -0.04 m; -0.10 .. +0.10 m:
n=770, FES2014 closer at 41%, median gain -0.00 m; +0.10 .. +0.25 m: n=44, FES2014 closer at 43%,
median gain -0.03 m; +0.25 .. +0.50 m: n=22, FES2014 closer at 50%, median gain +0.02 m; +0.50 ..
+0.75 m: n=3, FES2014 closer at 0%, median gain -0.64 m; +1.00 .. +inf m: n=5, FES2014 closer at
80%, median gain +1.43 m). Below about 1 m FES2014 is not the better atlas, so switching there does
harm; only above about 1 m does it reliably fix FES2022. D therefore must sit above the largest
difference at which FES2014 is wrong (Trondheim, 1.08 m) and below the smallest at which FES2022 is
badly wrong and must be caught (Port Alberni, 1.43 m). R is a secondary guard on large-tide coasts;
the known artefacts bound it from above.
