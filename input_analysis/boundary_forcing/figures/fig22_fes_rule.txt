The FES version rule (FES2014 where FES2022's MHWS exceeds it by a factor 1.5 and by 1.25 m) and its
growth to neighbours on the same artefact. It sets FES2014 at 66 stations (56 by the rule, 10 by the
growth). (a) At the 912 GESLA gauges: FES2022 and FES2014 MHWS minus the gauge's own; orange where
the rule would fire. (b) The FES2014 stations with a gauge within 150 km: both atlases' MHWS at the
station against the gauge's (circles: the rule, triangles: grown). (c) The FES2014 stations without
such a gauge, with the rule's boundary.

Reading: where the rule can be checked it is right: at the 3 gauges where it fires (Juneau, Skagway,
Port Alberni), FES2022 is 1.5-4.7 m too high and FES2014 within 0.1 m (0.6 m at Juneau). The flagged
stations near gauges sit with FES2014 at the gauge's level (Varangerfjord against Vardo 1.35 m, Le
Crouesty, Karachi for Sonmiani, Kukup, Port Stanley for the Falklands). Sao Marcos Bay: the Ponta da
Madeira gauge inside the bay reads 2.62 m, so the outer stations' FES2014 (2.1 m) is the plausible
value, not FES2022 (3.2-3.8 m). One flag is wrong: at the Saguenay mouth the Tadoussac gauge (2.18
m) sides with FES2022 (2.65 m) over FES2014 (1.38 m). Without gauges (c), most flags are FES2022
artefacts of known basins (Magellan, Gulf of California, Ungava), but some sit in real macrotidal
gulfs (Khambhat, Incheon) where neither atlas is checked.
