Reading: on the 2-4 m coasts the generator's neighbouring high waters lie on the gauges' (c, d),
where the HGRAPHER port and COAST-HG are 0.1-0.15 of the peak lower because their tide window is an
MHWS-level spring tide (test 7). On microtidal coasts (a, b) the peaks and neighbouring high waters
match, but all synthetic hydrographs drain lower between the high waters than the gauges, the
generator to about -0.15 of the peak and COAST-HG to -0.3, where the observed events stay near mean
sea level: the observed surge tail that holds the level up 30-48 h from the peak (fig09) is not in
their shapes.
