The GPD shape cap. (a) Maximum-likelihood shapes of the GTSM-on-FES skew surges at all stations and
of the gauges' own skew surges (test 9), with the tested caps (orange: production, 0.35). (b) RP100
level minus the gauge's convolution at the ratio gauges, r left one out, for each cap (non-TC- and
TC-dominated gauges; MAE solid, median dashed). (c) RP1000 at the stations with a shape above 0.2,
relative to production, against the station's shape.

Reading: the cap binds at 149 stations. At the gauges it makes no measurable difference up to RP100
(all gauges, MAE 22.4 cm without a cap, 22.6 cm at 0.35, 22.7 cm at 0.2): the gauges rarely sit
where GTSM's tail is heavy, and RP100 is still close to the data. The cap acts on RP1000 at the few
heavy-tailed stations: without it RP1000 rises by up to 9.7 m (0.5: 4.1 m), and 0.2 lowers it by up
to 3.1 m. The gauges' own shapes (median 0.01, standard deviation 0.25) spread more widely than
GTSM's (-0.03, 0.19), so they do not fix a cap; 0.35 bounds the extreme cases only.
