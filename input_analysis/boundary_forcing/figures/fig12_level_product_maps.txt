Reading: most of the coast moves by less than 0.2 m. The large departures are regional: lower in the
southern North Sea and Dover Strait, where GTSM's tide is too large and the FES tide replaces it;
lower in Hudson Bay, the Canadian Arctic and parts of Indonesia; higher in the western Baltic, on
the US Atlantic coast, in Japan and East China, where r is above 1.25, and in macrotidal basins
where GTSM's tide is too small (Ungava Bay, Amazon mouth, King Sound). At the gauges the new levels
are still below the gauge on TC coasts (fig14), so the increases there are not an overcorrection.
Uniform r marks coasts without 3 gauges within 500 km.
