The tropical-cyclone mask: at stations with a COAST-RP TC part, tidal cycles near an IBTrACS storm
leave the ETC fit, so a cyclone does not count twice (in the ERA5 surge record and in COAST-RP's
synthetic TC part). Bars: median of station minus gauge at RP10 and RP100 at the ratio gauges, r
left one out; ticks: MAE. (a) TC-dominated gauges, (b) gauges whose station has a TC part. Variants:
no mask; production (300 km, +-24 h, >= 34 kt); 150 and 500 km; +-12 and +-48 h; hurricane strength
(>= 64 kt) only.

Reading: the mask makes the TC coasts worse at the gauges. Without it the TC-dominated gauges' RP100
median is -3 cm and the MAE 36 cm; with production's mask -16 cm and 39 cm. A smaller radius (150
km) lies between; the time window and the wind threshold hardly matter. Without the mask a cyclone
counts in the ERA5 record and again in COAST-RP's TC part, yet the levels match the gauges better:
both TC sources are too low (GTSM-ERA5 resolves cyclones coarsely, COAST-RP's TC part is 0.2-0.25 m
below the gauges, §8.1), and the double count makes up for it. Removing it is right in principle and
wrong on the gauges until test 10 (STORM-forced GTSM) replaces the TC part.
