Black cross: no raise; blue circles: a shift added to COAST-RP_TC; green diamonds: a factor on COAST-RP_TC above its own RP2 level (its tide floor). (d) The shift per basin (number of TC-dominated gauges in brackets): fitted on the basin's own gauges (filled circle, 5-95% range of 2,000 bootstrap resamples of the gauges), fitted on the other basins' gauges (hollow triangle), and the one value fitted on all basins that production applies (dashed line). Shifts are tried from 0 to 0.4 m, so the bootstrap ranges stop at those bounds.
