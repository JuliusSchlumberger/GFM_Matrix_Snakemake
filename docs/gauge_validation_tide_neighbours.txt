Correcting the FES tide only with tide gauges reached through water (production) improves the fjord coasts and makes no region worse; through GEBCO water the gain comes with failures where its cells close inlets. (a) Ratio of each product station's tide factor with gauges reached through water outside the OSM land polygons (production) to the factor with all gauges within 25 km; the 1231 of 23226 stations where it changes, larger changes drawn on top (blue: lowered, orange: raised); Equal Earth projection. (b) RP100 mean absolute error at the 618 ratio gauges (gauge site left out of the tide factor), for all gauges, the regions where the rules differ and gauges with a spring tidal range above 4 m. (c) Mean absolute relative error of the corrected FES tide amplitude (sum of M2, S2, N2, K1 and O1) at the usable GESLA tide gauges, each with its own site left out. Neighbour rules: all gauges within 25 km (orange square), through GEBCO 15-arcsecond water below 0 m (vermilion triangle), through water outside the OSM land polygons on 100 m cells (blue circle, production), and gauges whose FES amplitude is within a factor 1.2 of the point's (pink diamond).
